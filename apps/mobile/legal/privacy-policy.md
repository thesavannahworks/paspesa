## Who we are

The Savannah Works makes and runs Paspesa and is the **data controller** for the personal data described here: we decide why and how it is used. You can reach us at [thesavannahworks@gmail.com](mailto:thesavannahworks@gmail.com).

## What Paspesa is

Paspesa is a **simulator** of mobile-money transactions. Balances, transactions and messages in Paspesa are practice records: **no real money moves when you simulate**, and Paspesa is not M-PESA. The only real money involved is what you choose to pay us for Paspesa tokens or for a single simulation (see the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md)).

**Paspesa never asks for your real M-PESA PIN.** The Paspesa PIN you create is a separate practice PIN; please do not reuse your real M-PESA PIN for it.

## What we collect

**When you sign in and set up your account**

- Your **email address**, to send you one-time sign-in codes and to identify your account on any device.
- The **name** and **phone number** you enter at setup, which appear in your simulated messages and statements.
- The **opening balance** you choose (a practice amount).
- Which version of this policy and the Terms you accepted, and when.

**When you use the simulator**

- Your **simulated transactions**: type, amount, fees, balances, date and time, and the transaction code.
- The details you type for the other party, such as a **recipient's name and phone number**, a till or paybill number, or an account reference. If you pick someone from your phone's contacts, only the name and number you pick are used, as if you had typed them.
- The **messages** Paspesa generates for each transaction, your **favourites**, and your recharge sender details.

**When you pay us (real money)**

- The **M-PESA phone number** that receives the payment prompt, the **amount**, the payment status, and the reference from the payment provider.
- Your **Paspesa token** balance and its history (tokens bought, granted, spent, set aside on a phone, returned or expired).

**About your devices**

- A random **installation ID** created by the app, the type of device (Android or web), when it last connected, and when you signed in or out on it.
- Our server's logs record technical details of each request, such as the time, the address it came from (IP address) and the outcome. We use them only to run and secure the service.

**When you contact us**

- What you write to us and your email address.

## What stays on your phone

Some things never leave your phone:

- Your **Paspesa PIN**. Only a scrambled (hashed) form is stored, and only on your phone. A new phone asks you to create a PIN.
- **Biometrics** (fingerprint or face). The phone checks them itself; Paspesa never receives biometric data.
- Your **profile picture**.
- Your **contacts list**. Paspesa reads it only when you open the contact picker and never uploads it.
- Whether you have read, starred or deleted a message, and app preferences such as the theme.

The camera is used only while you scan a QR code or take a profile picture. Pictures you choose from your gallery are used for the same two purposes only.

## Why we use your data

We use your data only for these purposes, each with its legal basis:

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Signing you in, keeping your account and backing up your simulations so you can restore them on another device | Providing the service you asked for |
| Selling tokens, taking payments and preventing double charges | Providing the service; keeping financial records |
| Preventing misuse, such as fraud, abuse of free tokens, or using Paspesa to deceive others | Our legitimate interest in a safe service |
| Answering your questions and requests | Providing the service |
| Keeping records the law requires, such as payment records | Legal obligation |
| Storing data with our providers outside Kenya | Your consent (see [Data stored outside Kenya](#data-stored-outside-kenya)) |

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## Data stored outside Kenya

Our providers store and process data outside Kenya: our database is hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), and our other providers may process data in the European Union or the United States. The Data Protection Act allows this with proof of adequate safeguards or with your consent (section 25(h)). We choose established providers that protect data with encryption and access controls, and **by accepting this policy when you sign in, you consent to this storage outside Kenya**. You can withdraw that consent by deleting your account.

## How long we keep data

| Data | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Your account, simulations, messages, favourites and tokens | Until you delete your account |
| Your email address and sign-in history | Until you delete your account |
| One-time sign-in codes (stored scrambled) | Until you delete your account; each code works once and only for 10 minutes |
| Records of real payments (amount, date, status, provider reference) | As long as the law requires us to keep financial records, even after you delete your account; your phone number is removed from them when you delete your account |
| Records of what our administrators did (audit log) | Kept to protect the service; personal details in them are removed when you delete your account |
| Server logs | Kept by our hosting provider for a limited time, then deleted automatically |

When personal data is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous (section 39).

## How we protect your data

- Connections to Paspesa are encrypted (HTTPS).
- Sign-in uses one-time codes sent to your email; codes are stored only in scrambled form and lock after 5 wrong tries.
- Each phone has its own secret key; our server stores only a scrambled form of it.
- Our server checks every simulation and payment itself. It never trusts a balance or a price sent by a phone, and payments are confirmed with the payment provider.
- Our administrators need a password and a second sign-in step (an authenticator app). Their access depends on their role, and what they do is recorded.
- If a breach puts your personal data at real risk, we will notify the Data Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and inform you in writing (section 43).

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to fix problems quickly.

## Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

- **be informed** of how your personal data is used (this policy);
- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **correct** data that is false, misleading, out of date or incomplete. You can change your name and phone number yourself in **Profile → Account Details**;
- **delete** your data. You can delete your account yourself in **Profile → Account Details → Delete my account**;
- **object** to the processing of all or part of your data, and ask us to **restrict** it;
- **receive your data** in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and have it sent to another service (data portability). We respond within 30 days;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time. This does not affect what was done before you withdrew it;
- **complain** to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (see below).

To use a right that the app does not offer directly, email [thesavannahworks@gmail.com](mailto:thesavannahworks@gmail.com) from the address you sign in with, so we can confirm it is you. We answer as soon as we can, and within 30 days.

## Children

Paspesa is meant for people aged **18 and over**. Under the Data Protection Act, a child's personal data may be processed only with a parent's or guardian's consent (section 33). If you are under 18, use Paspesa only with your parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child is using Paspesa without that consent, write to us and we will delete the account.

## What we never do

- Ask for or store your **real M-PESA PIN**.
- Move real money when you simulate.
- Sell your data or use it for advertising.
- Upload your contacts list, your biometrics, your profile picture or your Paspesa PIN.
