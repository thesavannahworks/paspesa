# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 25, 2024

## Introduction

Paspesa ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile-money transaction simulator. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web platform.

**Paspesa is a simulation platform.** No real money is transferred through our system, and we never ask for your actual M-PESA PIN or access to your real mobile money accounts.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

When you sign up for Paspesa, we collect:

- Email address (for authentication and account recovery)
- Phone number (simulated account identifier)
- Account name (chosen by you)
- Profile picture (optional, stored locally on your device)

### Simulation Data

We store your simulated transactions, including:

- Simulated account balance and transaction history
- Simulated contacts and phone numbers you interact with
- Paspesa Token balance and usage history
- Paspesa PIN (encrypted and never transmitted in plain text)

### Technical Information

- Device information and operating system
- App version and usage statistics
- IP address and connection logs

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain the Paspesa simulation service
- Authenticate your identity and secure your account
- Send you verification codes and important notifications
- Sync your simulation data across devices
- Process Paspesa Token purchases (via PayHero)
- Improve our service and develop new features
- Comply with legal obligations

## Real Payments

While Paspesa is a simulator, **Paspesa Tokens are purchased with real money** through our payment processor, PayHero. When you make a purchase:

- Your payment is processed securely by PayHero (not by Paspesa)
- We receive a transaction confirmation but never see your payment credentials
- PayHero's privacy policy applies to your payment information

## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using:

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
- Industry-standard database security on Neon PostgreSQL
- Hashed and salted passwords and PINs
- Regular security updates and monitoring

While we implement strong security measures, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

**We do not sell your personal information.** We may share your data only in these limited circumstances:

- With PayHero to process token purchases
- With SendGrid to deliver authentication emails
- With service providers who help us operate Paspesa (under strict confidentiality agreements)
- When required by law or to protect our legal rights
- With your explicit consent

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal information
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your account and data
- Export your simulation data
- Withdraw consent at any time
- Object to data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email provided at the end of this policy.

## Children's Privacy

Paspesa is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

## International Users

Paspesa is primarily designed for users in Kenya. If you access Paspesa from outside Kenya, your data may be transferred to and stored in Kenya or other jurisdictions where our service providers operate.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through the app. Your continued use of Paspesa after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
