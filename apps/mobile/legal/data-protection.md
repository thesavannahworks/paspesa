## Data controller

**The Savannah Works**, which runs Paspesa, is the data controller. Contact for anything about your personal data: [thesavannahworks@gmail.com](mailto:thesavannahworks@gmail.com).

## The principles we follow

Section 25 of the Act sets out how personal data must be handled. In Paspesa this means:

- **Lawful, fair and transparent:** we tell you what we do with your data in plain words (this notice and the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md)).
- **Only for stated purposes:** running your account, backups, tokens and payments, and keeping the service safe. Never advertising.
- **Only what is needed:** the contacts list, profile picture, PIN and biometrics stay on your phone and are never sent to us.
- **Accurate:** you can correct your name and phone number at any time.
- **Kept no longer than needed:** see [How long we keep data](privacy-policy.md#how-long-we-keep-data) (section 39).
- **Secure:** see [Security and data breaches](#security-and-data-breaches).
- **Transfers outside Kenya** only with safeguards or your consent: see [Transfers outside Kenya](#transfers-outside-kenya).

We also build privacy into Paspesa's design (section 41): for example, the server never receives your PIN, and a deleted account leaves no personal trace in its records.

## Your consent

When you sign in, you tick a box to accept the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service and this notice. We record **which version you accepted and when**, because the law asks us to be able to show your consent (section 32).

You can **withdraw your consent at any time** by deleting your account or by writing to us. Withdrawing does not make what was done before unlawful.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, while online:

1. Open **Profile → Account Details** and tap **Delete my account**.
2. Read what will happen and enter your Paspesa PIN.
3. Enter the 6-digit code we email to you. It proves the request comes from the account's owner.

The deletion is immediate and permanent:

- **Erased:** your simulated transactions, balances, messages, statements and favourites; your name, phone number and email address; your sign-in history and codes; your token wallet and its history; any simulations held for review.
- **Signed out:** every device linked to the account. The phone you deleted from is cleared.
- **Kept without your phone number:** records of real payments you made to us (amount, date, status and the payment provider's reference), because we must keep financial records. The record that the account was deleted is kept too, without personal details.
- **Not refunded:** unused tokens.

If a payment is still in progress, wait for it to finish before deleting. You can then sign in again with the same email address and start a new, empty account.

If you cannot use the app, write to us from the email address of the account and we will delete it for you.

## Getting a copy of your data

Write to [thesavannahworks@gmail.com](mailto:thesavannahworks@gmail.com) from the email address you sign in with and ask for a copy of your data. Within **30 days** we send you your account details, simulated transactions, messages, favourites, token history and payment records in a **structured, machine-readable format** (JSON or CSV). If you ask, we send it directly to another service where that is technically possible (section 38).

## How we answer requests

- To protect you, we act only on requests from the email address of the account, or after confirming in another way that the request is yours.
- We answer as soon as we can, and within 30 days.
- Requests are free. If a request is clearly unfounded or excessive, we may refuse it and will tell you why.
- When we correct or delete data at your request, we tell the providers that hold it, where that is needed (section 40).

## Children

Paspesa is meant for adults. A child's personal data may be processed only with the consent of a parent or guardian (section 33), and a parent or guardian exercises the child's rights for them (section 27). If you are a parent or guardian and want a child's account deleted, write to us.

## Processors

Our service providers process personal data **only on our instructions**, under their contracts with us (section 42): Railway (server), Neon (database), Vercel (web app), SendGrid (email) and PayHero (payments). The [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md#who-else-handles-your-data) says what each one does. Safaricom handles the M-PESA payments you approve under its own terms.

## Transfers outside Kenya

Our database is hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), and our other providers may process data in the European Union or the United States. Section 25(h) allows this when there are adequate safeguards or with your consent. We rely on both:

- **Safeguards:** established providers that encrypt data in transit and at rest, restrict access to it, and process it only for us;
- **Consent:** you agree to these transfers when you accept the Privacy Policy at sign-in.

## Security and data breaches

We protect personal data with encrypted connections, scrambled (hashed) storage of codes and device keys, server-side checks of every simulation and payment, and two-step sign-in, roles and an activity record for our administrators.

If a breach puts personal data at real risk, we will:

- notify the **Data Commissioner within 72 hours** of becoming aware of it (section 43);
- tell the people affected **in writing** as soon as reasonably possible, with what happened, what it means for them and what they can do;
- fix the cause and record what we did.

## Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you based only on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects (section 35). Automatic checks, such as whether a simulation matches the rules or whether a payment was confirmed, can hold a simulation for review; a person can look at it, and you can write to us about it.
